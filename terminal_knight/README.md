# ⚔️ Terminal Knight

**Terminal Knight** is a real-time, terminal-based RPG where you build a legendary hero, battle waves of monsters, and gear up with powerful equipment to survive the dungeon.

## 🚀 Getting Started

### Installation
1. Ensure you have **Python 3.x** installed.
2. Install the required dependencies:
   ```bash
   pip install rich
   ```
3. Run the game:
   ```bash
   python main.py
   ```

---

## 🕹️ How to Play

### 🛡️ Choosing Your Class
At the start, you'll choose one of three archetypes:
- **Warrior**: High Strength and Constitution. Excels at taking hits and dealing massive physical damage.
- **Mage**: High Intelligence. Uses elemental power to destroy enemies from a distance.
- **Rogue**: High Agility. Fast attacks, high critical hit chance, and superior dodge capabilities.

### 🏰 The Hero's Lodge (Lobby)
The Lodge is your home base between runs. From here, you can:
- **`b`**: Start a **Battle Run**.
- **`s`**: Visit the **Shop** to buy gear or upgrade your skills.
- **`e`**: Open your **Equipment** menu to manage your gear.
- **`c`**: Open your **Character Sheet** to view your total stats and milestones.
- **`q`**: Quit and save your progress.

### ⚔️ Combat (The Battle Run)
Combat is **real-time**. Your hero attacks automatically based on their **Agility**.

- **Attack Speed**: The higher your Agility, the faster you strike.
- **Abilities**: Press **`a`** to unleash your class-specific ability (has a cooldown).
- **Consumables**: Press **`h`** to use a healing potion (if you have one in your inventory).
- **Retreat**: Press **`r`** to retreat to the lodge and keep your loot.
- **Waves**: Every monster you kill grants gold and XP. Every 10th wave is a Boss fight!

### 🛒 The Shop & Equipment
- **Skill Upgrades**: Spend gold to increase your Strength, Agility, Intelligence, or Constitution. This increases your base power and grants a multiplier to XP gain for that skill.
- **Gear**: Buy weapons, armor, and accessories. Gear is **level-locked**, so you'll need to grow stronger to use the best equipment.
- **Rarity**: Items can be **Common**, **Rare**, **Epic**, or **Legendary**. Higher rarity items provide significantly better bonuses.
- **Set Bonuses**: Equipping 3 or more items from the same set (e.g., *World-Ender*) grants powerful unique passives.

---

## 💎 Pro Tips
- **Synergy**: If you're a Mage, focus on Intelligence to boost both your attack power and your HP regeneration.
- **Looting**: Not all loot is worth keeping. If your inventory is full, sell old gear in the shop to make room for Legendary drops.
- **Milestones**: Reaching certain skill levels unlocks permanent milestones that provide massive boosts to your hero.

**Good luck, Knight! May your blade stay sharp and your health bar full.**
